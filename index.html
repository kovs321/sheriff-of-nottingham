01 / IDENTITY

THE KING’S LAW.
THE PEOPLE’S BURDEN.

In Disney’s Robin Hood, the Sheriff of Nottingham is a hulking grey wolf charged with keeping order for Prince John. In practice, that means squeezing every last farthing from the people of Nottingham.

He is boastful, greedy and convinced of his own cleverness—yet Robin Hood repeatedly slips past him in plain sight. The Sheriff is menacing enough to be feared and foolish enough to be funny.

01
THE COLLECTION

Taxes without mercy

With Trigger and Nutsy beside him, the Sheriff raids homes, searches hiding places and takes coins even from Nottingham’s poorest families.

02
THE HUNT

Outfoxed in Sherwood

He relentlessly hunts Robin Hood and Little John, but his certainty that he can spot any disguise becomes one of his greatest weaknesses.

03
THE CHURCH

A line finally crossed

When he steals from the poor box and arrests Friar Tuck, Prince John uses the capture to bait Robin into a rescue at the castle.

04
THE SENTENCE

Authority revoked

King Richard returns, restores justice and condemns the Sheriff—alongside Prince John and Sir Hiss—to hard labour in the Royal Rock Pile.
