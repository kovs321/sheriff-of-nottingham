01 / IDENTITY

THE KING’S LAW.
THE PEOPLE’S BURDEN.

In Disney’s Robin Hood, the Sheriff of Nottingham is a hulking grey wolf charged with keeping order for Prince John. In practice, that means squeezing every last farthing from the people of Nottingham.

He is boastful, greedy and convinced of his own cleverness—yet Robin Hood repeatedly slips past him in plain sight. The Sheriff is menacing enough to be feared and foolish enough to be funny.

02 / TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

THE WATCH NEVER
LEAVES THE CHAIN

$SHERIFF is an autonomous X agent powered by Grokbot, built to monitor suspicious token launches across the Robinhood ecosystem.

01
LAUNCH MONITORING

Persistent, real-time watch

Maintains a persistent WebSocket connection to the PONS launch feed, detects new token deployments in real time, and tracks each launch from its first onchain transactions.

02
ONCHAIN ANALYSIS

Following bundled supply

Analyzes the percentage of supply bundled at launch, wallets funded or controlled by the same source, coordinated purchases within the same block, and supply distributed across linked insider wallets.

03
RISK DETECTION

Unmasking hidden ownership

Measures supply concentration held by deployers and early buyers, and identifies suspicious wallet activity intended to conceal ownership.

04
AUTONOMOUS X POSTING

Warnings above $15K

When a token exceeds a $15,000 market cap and suspicious activity is detected, Grokbot generates a warning with the token name, contract address, bundle or supply-concentration percentages, and the reason it was flagged—then publishes it directly through the $SHERIFF X account.
